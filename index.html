Stage 4

How the AI decides, in three steps

The technique is k-nearest neighbours (k-NN): turn everything into numbers, then pick what is closest. Omweg does it twice. Every step below works on the request from stage 2, so it changes when you type something else.

A

Your words become numbers

An AI agent turns your sentence into criteria.

About the AI · 1/3In a real version a language model (LLM) reads your sentence. Here simple rules do it, so every step can be checked.
B

The nearest day package is chosen

Eight day packages, each described by numbers. The closest one wins: shorter bar, better fit.

C

Each stop gets the nearest real place

For each stop, all 54 places are compared at your arrival time. The closest open place wins.

About the AI · 2/3This part runs for real: k-NN computes every distance live in your browser, on our database of places.

Report §2.1

k-NN, twice

Steps B and C. No training: distances are computed when you ask, so a new café only needs a row in the database.

Report §2.1

Scaled numbers

All criteria are standardised (z-scores), so euros don't outweigh hours. Few criteria per step keeps distances meaningful.

Report §2.2

Bias and local places

Crowd timing and a preference for local businesses counter the popularity bias of normal apps (stage 3). A local business is small, independent and not a headline attraction (crowd at most 3).
